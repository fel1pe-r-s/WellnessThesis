# Gemini Content Generation Guide for WellnessThesis

Use these specialized prompts to ensure your blog content is scientifically accurate, engaging, and optimized for your premium wellness platform.

## 1. Post Idea Generator
**Prompt:**
> "I need 5 unique and compelling blog post ideas for my premium wellness platform 'WellnessThesis'. Our audience values biohacking, metabolic health, longevity science, and premium beauty. The ideas should feel like cutting-edge research transformed into practical insights. For each idea, provide a catchy title, a short summary (the 'discovery'), and 3 key scientific points to cover."

## 2. Copywriting & Article Drafting
**Prompt:**
> "Act as a high-end wellness science communicator. Draft a full blog post for 'WellnessThesis' about **[INSERT TOPIC HERE]**. 
> 
> **Structure:**
> - **Premium Title:** Hook the reader immediately.
> - **The Discovery:** A bold introductory paragraph about a modern health insight.
> - **Scientific Breakdown:** 3-4 sections detailing the 'why' behind the wellness.
> - **Practical Protocol:** A bulleted list of actionable steps the reader can take.
> - **Call to Action:** A natural transition to recommend a product related to this topic.
> 
> **Tone:** Authoritative yet accessible, premium, and science-backed. Use rich, descriptive language."

## 3. Image Prompt Generation
**Prompt:**
> "Generate 3 detailed DALL-E/Midjourney prompts for a blog post about **[INSERT TOPIC HERE]**. The images should have a premium, clean, medical-spa aesthetic. Think high-end laboratory meets organic nature, macro shots, or minimalist wellness environments. Avoid generic stock photo looks; focus on vibrant HSL colors, glassmorphism elements, or professional architectural photography."

## 4. Newsletter Hook
**Prompt:**
> "Create a short, punchy newsletter email to promote our latest post about **[POST TITLE]**. Start with a 'Did you know?' hook about a hidden health benefit, summarize the post in 2 sentences, and include a bold call to action to read the full insight on the WellnessThesis inner circle."

---

### Tips for Best Results:
- **Be Specific:** The more details you give Gemini about the specific research or product you want to feature, the better the output.
- **Iterate:** If the first draft feels too generic, ask: *"Make this sound more professional and elite, focusing on the longevity aspect."*
- **Verify:** Always double-check any medical claims with actual research before publishing.
